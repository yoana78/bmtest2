## Brand & Style
This design system defines an institutional, high-precision identity for premium pet nutrition cold-chain logistics, certified warehousing, and global B2B distribution. The product sits at the intersection of clinical biological care and enterprise supply-chain telemetry.

### Emotional Profile & Brand Personality
- **Clinical Precision:** Conveys pharmaceutical-grade integrity, certified freshness, and unbroken cold-chain compliance.
- **Enterprise Reliability:** Instills confidence across enterprise distributors, veterinary networks, and direct-to-retail brands.
- **Pure & Pristine:** Evokes cleanliness and humane care through deep whites, cool arctic tones, and spacious layouts.

### Design Style: Modern Corporate Glassmorphism
The aesthetic blends precision enterprise SaaS with subtle frosted-glass telemetry. Interfaces use crisp semi-transparent card panels, hairline slate boundaries, micro-diffused indigo-tinted drop shadows, and high-legibility geometric typography.

## Colors
The palette balances vibrant signature blue logistics branding with sterile, high-end white surfaces and deep architectural navy.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#0066B3`):** Signature Arctic Logistic Blue. Applied to primary calls-to-action, active tracking states, core navigation highlights, and telemetry vectors.
- **Secondary / Deep Slate (`#0D1B2A`):** Deep Abyssal Navy. Anchors display typography, critical data thresholds, sidebar navigation, and primary text contrast.
- **Tertiary / Cyan Accent (`#0284C7`):** Active telemetry, live sensor nodes, route paths, and real-time freshness indices.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Cool slate gray for secondary typography, table dividers, column headers, and structural outlines.

### Surface System
- **Base Background:** `#F8FAFF` (Ultra-light icy white).
- **Elevated Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white).
- **Subtle Well / Inset:** `#F0F4F8` (Crisp structural fill for data tables, metrics panels, and cold-storage status clusters).

### Semantic & Feedback Roles
- **Optimal Freshness / Safe Range:** `#059669` (Emerald Emerald-600).
- **Temperature Deviation / Warning:** `#D97706` (Amber-600).
- **Cold-Chain Breach / Critical Alert:** `#DC2626` (Crimson-600).
- **Information / Telemetry Ping:** `#0284C7` (Sky-600).

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the single typographic family across all applications. Its contemporary geometry, wide counters, and refined rounded joints lend warmth to high-density logistics tables, dispatch manifests, and telemetry dashboards without sacrificing legibility.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used for critical freight counts, operational KPIs, and high-impact portal entry screens. Requires tighter negative letter-spacing to maintain cohesion.
- **Section & Title Roles:** Used for distribution hub names, cargo manifests, and lot batch IDs.
- **Body:** Built for dense tabular reads, shipping bills, route itineraries, and SLA audit logs.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Tracked-out uppercase and small semi-bold designations for temperature status, SKU tracking codes, and cold-chain compliance stamps.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system is founded on an 8-point structural grid with 4-point micro-adjustments for telemetry indicators and inline data readouts.

### Breakpoints & Fluid Grid
- **Mobile (up to 767px):** 4 fluid columns, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Cards stack vertically; complex inventory tables collapse into swipeable cards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 fluid columns, 32px margins, 20px gutters. Dual-pane layout for manifests and real-time routing.
- **Desktop (1024px – 1440px+):** 12 fluid columns, 48px margins, 24px gutters, max-width pinned to 1440px. Supports persistent side-dock telemetry, warehouse routing maps, and dense inventory data grids side-by-side.

### Spacing Philosophy
Generous white margins offset high-density inventory data, ensuring operational views feel uncluttered, airy, and clinical rather than industrial.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is established through translucent white frosted surfaces, crisp cool-slate hairline borders, and cool tinted ambient shadows.

### Glassmorphic Layers
- **Surface Floating / In-Flight Glass:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Used for sticky headers, telemetry overlays, and shipment drawer panels.
- **Surface Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F8FAFF` background with a subtle border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.

### Ambient Shadow Scales
- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Low Card):** `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.04)`. Used for base grid items and list rows.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Metrics Card):** `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 102, 179, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.04)`. Introduces a slight primary-tint glow.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Flight Telemetry Drawers):** `0 16px 32px -4px rgba(13, 27, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 102, 179, 0.06)`. Used for floating dispatch panels, filter overlays, and global modals.

## Shapes
The system relies on smooth, friendly modern radii (Level 2: 0.5rem default, 1rem for `rounded-lg`, and 1.5rem for `rounded-xl`), creating a warm, polished, and contemporary feel that differentiates this platform from harsh, angular industrial shipping tools.

### Applied Radii
- **Input Fields, Badges, Micro-chips:** `rounded-md` (6px - 8px) for crisp control definitions.
- **Standard Cards, Data Blocks, Telemetry Hubs:** `rounded-lg` (16px) paired with 1px slate-200 boundary borders.
- **Hero Metrics, Modal Dialogs, Floating Drawers:** `rounded-xl` (24px) for prominent, elevated panels.
- **Status Pills, Tracking Waypoints, Avatar Rings:** Full pill (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0066B3` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-lg` (12px), `font-weight: 600`. Hover brings brightness lift to `#0077D4` with `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 102, 179, 0.25)` elevation.
- **Secondary / Ghost Blue:** Transparent surface, `1.5px solid #0066B3`, text `#0066B3`. On hover: subtle `#F0F7FD` tint.
- **Tertiary / Subdued:** `#F0F4F8` background, `#0D1B2A` text, no border. On hover: `#E2E8F0`.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Cold-Chain / Temperature Optimal:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#ECFDF5` background, `#059669` text, `1px solid rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.2)`. Includes a pulse indicator dot.
- **Transit Node Status:** `#EFF6FF` background, `#0066B3` text, `1px solid rgba(0, 102, 179, 0.15)`.

### Data Cards
- Container built from `#FFFFFF` with `rounded-xl` (24px) perimeter and `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Padding uses `space-lg` (24px).
- Internal sectioning uses horizontal separator lines in `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)`.
- Metric numbers are set in `headline-lg` or `display-lg` using Abyssal Navy (`#0D1B2A`).

### Inputs & Field Selectors
- Height: 44px for standard inputs.
- Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`, `rounded-md` (8px).
- Focus State: Smooth transition to `border-color: #0066B3` along with a 3px ring of `rgba(0, 102, 179, 0.15)`.
- Label typography: `label-md` in `#1E293B` positioned cleanly above the field with 6px spacing.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkbox: 18x18px, `rounded-sm` (4px), `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Selected state is solid `#0066B3` with a white checkmark.
- Radio: 18x18px concentric circle, inset active dot `#0066B3` with 2px white border ring.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Cold-Chain Telemetry Graph:** Clean line chart using `#0066B3` for target temperature curves and an amber threshold envelope (`#D97706`) with frosted tooltip overlays.
- **Route / Waypoint Stepper:** Vertical or horizontal path connected by 2px hairline strokes in `#E2E8F0`. Completed stops render in solid blue `#0066B3`; active shipment hubs display an illuminated halo pulse.
